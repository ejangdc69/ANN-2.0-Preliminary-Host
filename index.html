RESEARCH TRANSPARENCY

Research methodology.

A clear record of the source data, model-selection procedure, equation, and unresolved limitations.

DATA DEFINITION

What is predicted?

Contractor-reported estimates of construction delay days specifically attributed to extreme heat and occurring within the selected month. Questionnaire response choices included 0, 1–5, and greater than 5 days; the 507 usable V4 records contain 1–5-day values.

MODEL INPUTS

What drives predictions?

Three categorical selections—month, construction activity code, municipality/city—one-hot encoded into 35 features. Temperature, heat index, humidity, and heat-exposure hours are not direct inputs in the selected V2.3 network.

MODEL SELECTION

How was it trained?

Project-disjoint train/validation/test partitions: 355 / 76 / 76 rows. Forty candidates compared using the validation partition; 12-neuron ReLU MLP, α=1, seed=42 selected. The deployment artifact was subsequently fitted on all 507 V4 observations.

LIMITATIONS

What remains unverified?

Contractor estimates have not yet been independently reconciled to the researcher's three official Monthly Progress Reports. Repeated monthly answer patterns warrant scrutiny; geography and seasonal coverage are limited. Final-release unbiased predictive accuracy remains unestablished.

MODEL ARTIFACT

Exact neural-network coefficients

For reproducibility, this public site includes the numerical JSON parameters of the fitted ANN. No raw questionnaire responses, contractor identifiers, or MPR documents are included.
